Ready to Order?

Please include the following when sending your enquiry:

  • Theme or colours
  • Inspiration pictures (if available)

Ordering Policy

  • Cakes and large orders must be placed 1–2 weeks in advance.
  • Other sweet treats require 3–5 days' notice.
  • Rush orders are subject to an additional fee.
  • A 50% non-refundable deposit is required for cakes and large orders.
  • The remaining balance must be paid 3–5 days before collection or delivery.
  • Full payment is required when ordering other sweet treats.
  • Delivery is available at an additional cost.
  • No refunds will be given for late cancellations.
  • Please collect your order at the agreed time.
