About Us

Our Story

Sweet Treats by Abigail is a home bakery based in Booysens Park, Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth). We are passionate about creating beautiful, delicious desserts that bring joy to every celebration.

From elegant custom cakes to everyday sweet treats, each order is carefully prepared with attention to detail and a commitment to quality. Whether it's a once-in-a-lifetime event or a simple family gathering, we're here to make every moment sweeter.

Our Mission

To provide beautifully crafted desserts that taste just as amazing as they look while delivering friendly, reliable customer service.
